I. INTRODUCTION


THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING AGREEMENT, executed on this date between the SAINT JOHNS COUNTY SHERIFFS OFFICE; hereafter referred to as "SJSO" and the REPRESENTATIVE of an ORGANIZATION or RESIDENCE,

hereafter referred to as "PARTNER"; collectively referred to as "THE PARTNERS."


II. PREAMBLES


WHEREAS, SJSO is a law enforcement organization responsible for law enforcement services in the geographic area in which the SJSO has jurisdiction.


WHEREAS, the PARTNER is the representative of an organization or residence within the jurisdiction of the SJSO.


WHEREAS, this AGREEMENT has as its objective the collaboration and participation of both organizations with the mutual intent to reduce crime, deter crime and reduce the fear of crime in furtherance of the law enforcement mission to protect life and property. This AGREEMENT facilitates the establishment of channels of communication and sharing of video sources that permit the creation and interchange of information, as well as collaboration with the expressed goal of providing enhanced responsiveness and situational awareness for SJSO on behalf of the PARTNER.


WHEREAS, the missions of the PARTNERs are complementary;


THEREFORE, the PARTNERs wish to work together and in compliance with the following clauses:


III. GOAL


The goal of this AGREEMENT is to set forth guidelines for sharing of PARTNER's video sources with SJSO.


IV. PURPOSE


Video surveillance footage, which is owned by or under management by the PARTNER, will be provided at the discretion of the PARTNER to SJSO upon request. It is understood SJSO will have no responsibility or obligation to continuously monitor any activity captured by the PARTNER’s video sources. Video sharing by the PARTNER with SJSO is intended to be done in the spirit of

PARTNERSHIP for improved situational awareness and efficiency of emergency response by SJSO and other law enforcement agencies.


V. RESPONSIBILITIES OF SJSO


SJSO will not share video footage from the PARTNER’s camera views with members of the public, without the prior consent of the PARTNER.


Further, SJSO will ensure video access and viewing is strictly limited to authorized personnel.


SJSO understands the sharing of video sources is at the discretion of the PARTNER and the PARTNER remains Custodian of Records over video sources.


VI. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTNER


At the PARTNER’s discretion, the PARTNER agrees to grant SJSO access to any recorded and/or archived footage to enable SJSO to perform its obligations outlined in this AGREEMENT.


The PARTNER understands this program is not a replacement for any emergency assistance or other 911 services. In case of any emergencies or immediate danger situations, the PARTNER must

contact 911 and/or other appropriate authorities.


VII. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY


The PARTNER understands under no circumstance shall SJSO have any liability whatsoever for:


a. any illicit, illegal or criminal activity that may take place at or near the surveilled premises, even if

captured by the PARTNER’s video sources;


b. any injury, theft, damages or other losses that may result from any such illegal or criminal activity

which may take place at or near the surveilled premises, even if captured by the Security System;


c. any costs or issues associated with the acquisition, operation, support, replacement or

management of the PARTNER’s internet connection, or security system.


The PARTNER’s participation in the PARTNERSHIP does not make the PARTNER an agent of

SJSO, and does not grant them any rights or duties of SJSO.


VIII. EFFECTIVE DATES AND TERM AGREEMENTS


This AGREEMENT will come to effect as of the date this document is acknowledged by the PARTNER, and will remain in force and effect until it is terminated by either party, with or without cause.
