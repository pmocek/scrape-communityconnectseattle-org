MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Providing for Access to the Owner’s Private Security Camera System

This Agreement for Providing for Access to the Security Camera System (hereinafter referred to as "Agreement” or “MOA”) is made and entered into by and between: the City of Memphis, through its Division of Police Services, (hereinafter referred to as "MPD"), and the camera system owner with its principal address indicated within the Registry portal, hereafter referred to as “OWNER”.

collectively referred to as the "Parties."

  1. WHEREAS, the OWNER has a security camera system on its premises or on a MPD/MLGW approved and permitted utility pole, and is desirous of allowing MPD to have access to the system as needed for crime prevention and investigative or other legitimate law enforcement purposes; and

  2. WHEREAS, access to OWNER’S security camera system may assist MPD with legitimate law enforcement activities within its jurisdiction, including but not limited to, conducting a threat assessment or investigating criminal activity at or near the OWNER’S premises; and



  1. WHEREAS, OWNER agrees to obtain and maintain a security camera system with certain technological standards (such as, sufficient Internet Service Provider band width, licensing requirements for viewing interface, lighting of the areas being filmed, and capability with the MPD’S RTCC technology) at OWNER’S sole cost and expense to allow MPD’s access to the video storage system; and

  2. WHEREAS, the video will be stored on a third party cloud storage platform, which both OWNER and MPD may access, and MPD will be given permission by OWNER to view both live streaming and recorded video from OWNER’S security camera system or video storage system (hard drive or cloud based) for viewing pre-recorded, saved video footage or actual live video footage (hereinafter collectively referred to as “Live Feeds”); and

  3. The Parties desire to enter into this MOA outlining their roles and responsibilities with regard to the sharing of OWNER’S security camera system video;

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual terms, conditions, promises, and covenants hereinafter set forth, the Parties agree as follows:

  1. The forgoing recitals are true and correct and are incorporated herein by reference.

  2. OWNER’s obligations:

    1. OWNER is solely responsible for the cost of acquiring, installing, and maintaining the OWNER’S security camera system as required by MPD, which shall be compatible with the technology and any video management system and cloud storage requirements of MPD’s Real Time Crime Center (“RTCC”) viewing systems/software. This may include obtaining and maintaining, any and all licenses charges by third parties, internet connectivity, cloud storage, and other related costs necessary to interface with RTCC technology viewing platform.

    2. OWNER agrees to allow all video to be uploaded and stored in a cloud based video management system, to which both OWNER and MPD will be able to access using their own log-in/passwords.

    3. OWNER is freely and voluntarily providing access to its video management system or video feeds to MPD, for MPD to view Live Feeds and/or recorded video. Participation is completely voluntary and OWNER’S participation can be withdrawn at any time, subject to conditions below.

    4. OWNER shall allow MPD to remotely access its security camera system for the sole purpose of public safety and legitimate law enforcement activities, training and testing of the system, and MPD’S demonstration to the press/public of the systems.

    5. All components of the security camera system and other paraphernalia relative to the system, including the video, are property of the OWNER.

    6. At no time is OWNER acting as an agent or employee of MPD.

    7. If the OWNER transfers ownership of the OWNER’S cameras/system, then OWNER will notify MPD immediately so that arrangements can be made by the new owner and MPD to assign this Agreement to the new owner, or to enter into a new agreement by and between MPD and the new owner, at MPD’S option.

    8. OWNER, at OWNER’S sole cost and expense, will maintain and update its security cameras/system and internet service provider speeds and cloud storage capabilities, to meet the minimum requirements specified by MPD to interface with the RTCC technology and allow for Live Feeds to be monitored by MPD. Said minimum technical requirements for a system are 5MB or more of bandwidth PER CAMERA being actively shared, in addition to other internet needs of the business. These minimum technical requirements may, from time to time, be updated, modified or changed by MPD and communicated by MPD to the installer at a later date because of any system equipment manufacturer’s changes in equipment performance standards. MPD makes no representations or guarantees that the quality of the OWNER’S existing camera feeds/footage will be viewable in the RTCC due to its age and/or older model of OWNER’S cameras and it is the OWNER’S responsibility to upgrade the same if needed.

    9. OWNER’S cameras accessible by the RTCC will be positioned to capture physical areas visited and in the public right of way and/or generally accessible by the public that are entering or exiting, outside or around the building structure containing the property . OWNER will make all reasonable efforts to ensure that there are no obstructing objects, fixtures, or signage limiting the view of any of OWNER’S security cameras accessible by the RTCC.

    10. OWNER will make all reasonable efforts to ensure that footage from all cameras accessible by the RTCC is stored, in preference of the Memphis Police Department , for fifteen (15) business days via a storage device or cloud-based storage that is compatible with RTCC video management systems.

    11. OWNER may install or affix MPD approved signage near the OWNER’S camera which is visible to the public, indicating that OWNER is collaborating with MPD and that the video footage from OWNER’S security camera systems may be accessed by MPD. MPD will provide such signage to OWNER for display for the duration of this Agreement. If, and when, this Agreement is terminated, the OWNER will remove and destroy such signage or, at the option of the MPD, MPD’S personnel may enter the OWNER’S property to retrieve said signage and remove it from the OWNER’S property.

    12. OWNER grants an irrevocable license to MPD to use the Live Video feeds as contemplated herein. OWNER retains full ownership over their private security camera system and all video footage maintained by OWNER’S system. OWNER will cooperate with MPD’S request for a copy of any video recorded by OWNER that is part of a criminal investigation, at no cost to MPD.

    13. OWNER agrees to allow MPD to release any video footage, through any means, including, but not limited to electronic transmission, traditional print, or electronic media, including social media, of an incident or related to an incident that is subject to criminal investigation. OWNER acknowledges that publicly releasing video footage without the permission of MPD could potentially hinder ongoing law enforcement or prosecution efforts to investigate, apprehend, and prosecute criminal suspects. Therefore, OWNER agrees to provide MPD with seven (7) business days, written advance notice at the addresses found in the ‘NOTICE’ section below, prior to disclosing or releasing any video footage that is the subject of a criminal investigation and/or prosecution to the media or any other third-party through any means, including, but not limited to electronic transmission, traditional print, or electronic media including social media. OWNER understands and acknowledges, that any video that is retained and stored by MPD becomes public records under Tennessee Law and may result in release of the video as required by law and/or at the MPD’S discretion at any time.

  3. MPD's obligations:

      1. MPD shall follow all policies and procedures applicable to the viewing, use, retention and storage of video, including but not limited to the court-ordered Modified Consent Decree. Accessing, viewing and use of the video will be used only for legitimate law enforcement purposes.

      2. MPD will provide the RTCC computers and other infrastructure utilized by the MPD’s personnel at the RTCC to gain access to the security camera system.

      3. MPD shall ensure that access to OWNER’S security camera system will be only by pre-authorized personnel, and will be accessed only for legitimate law enforcement purposes.

      4. MPD may provide OWNER with standard signage to be conspicuously displayed on the OWNER’S property or near the OWNER’S camera to identify that OWNER’s security camera system is linked to MPD’S RTCC for Live Feeds when activated and needed by MPD.

      5. MPD is not obligated to constantly monitor the Live Feeds from the OWNER’S security camera system.

      6. MPD in no way shall be considered a private security solution for the OWNER’S premises. This Agreement does not ensure, guarantee or warrant the prevention of crime or the timeliness of a response to an emergency by MPD or any other division of the City of Memphis.

  1. No Waiver of Public Duty Doctrine: The OWNER acknowledges, agrees, and understands that MPD’s access to OWNER’s Security Camera System as provided in this Agreement shall not be construed in any way as to limit the Public Duty Legal Doctrine/Defense, applicable to police and law enforcement agencies and their agents and employees, and that this Agreement does not constitute any type of special relationship or agency between the Parties. OWNER acknowledges, agrees, and understands that this Agreement creates no private cause of action against MPD, or any of MPD’s officers , employees, or agents, for failure to protect OWNER from harm.

  2. Liability: MPD is a political subdivision of the State of Tennessee as defined in Chapter §29-20-101 et. Seq., Tennessee Statutes, and shall be fully responsible for acts and omissions of its agents, contractors, or employees in the performance of its obligations under this Agreement, to the extent permitted by law. Nothing herein is intended to serve as a waiver of sovereign immunity by any party to which sovereign immunity may be applicable. Nothing herein shall be construed as consent by a state agency, or political subdivision, of the state of Tennessee to be sued by third parties, in any matter, arising out of this Agreement, or any other contract.

  3. No Compensation: OWNER shall not receive any compensation from MPD for allowing access to its security camera system. Each party will bear their own costs.

  4. Termination: This Agreement is evergreen, and may be terminated at any time, by the OWNER, for any reason or for convenience. This Agreement may be terminated at any time, by MPD if OWNER breaches this agreement, the Live Feed program is no longer being offered by MPD; the OWNER’S system is not compatible to MPD’S Live Feed program; OWNER transfers ownership of its installed security system; and/or at MPD’S discretion at any time.. Parties terminating this Agreement shall provide written notice to the other Party by way of the Notice section below. Upon termination of this Agreement, the MPD’S access to the OWNER’S security camera system shall cease. If OWNER elects to terminate this Agreement, OWNER should change all passwords, codes and security prompts to discontinue MPD’S RTCC accessibility to OWNER’S security camera system. The provisions contained in paragraphs 2(m), 2(n), 6, and 10, shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

  5. Technology Limitations: The OWNER understands that MPD’S telecommunication infrastructure at the RTCC and the OWNERS own system and internet service provider have finite ability to support simultaneous telecommunications and internet communications at one time. The OWNER understands that other third-party live streaming data and video feeds may have preferential contractual rights to the use and access of viewing of these feeds by MPD. MPD has no control over internet outages, RTCC service outages, power outages or other equipment outages. Therefore, MPD makes no guarantees or representations that the Live Feeds from the OWNER’S equipment and security cameras will be accessed or will be in service for any amount of time. MPD’S continued performance under this Agreement is subject to: (i) availability of network telecommunications capacity and system compatibility at RTCC; (ii) MPD’S workforce availability to set up and maintain the RTCC and these Live Feed systems contemplated herein; and/or (iii) noninterference with any other RTCC telecommunication systems or data feeds.

  6. Governing Law and Venue and Waiver of Jury Trial: This Agreement shall be interpreted and construed in accordance with and governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. Any controversies or legal problems arising out of this Agreement and any action involving the enforcement or interpretation of any rights hereunder shall be submitted to the jurisdiction of the Thirtieth Judicial District in and for Shelby County, Tennessee, and shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. TO ENCOURAGE PROMPT AND EQUITABLE RESOLUTION OF ANY LITIGATION THAT MAY ARISE HEREINUDER RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES ANY RIGHTS IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY OF SUCH LITIGATION AND THE MATTER SHALL BE EXCLUSIVELY HEARD AND DECIDED BY A JUDGE.

Signatures of the Parties transmitted by facsimile, e-mail or digital format shall be deemed to be their original signatures for all purposes.

The City, by execution of this Agreement, assumes no liability for damages caused to persons or property by reason of OWNER providing goods or services herein or for injury to any employee, agent, subcontractor, invitee, or customer of the OWNER.


  1. General Compliance with Laws. OWNER is assumed to be familiar with and agrees that at all times it will observe and comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, ordinances, and regulations in any manner affecting this Agreement. OWNER shall promptly notify City of any conflict discovered between the Agreement and any applicable laws, rules, regulations, and/or permits and licenses, and await resolution of the conflict.
