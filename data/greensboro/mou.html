Camera Registry Program Policy


The City of Greensboro and the Greensboro Police Department are dedicated to improving

the quality of life of residents of the City of Greensboro. Private security cameras installed

throughout the City may sometimes capture footage of emergencies or criminal activity.


Residents and business owners are invited to voluntarily register their video surveillance

systems with the Greensboro Police Department as part of the Connect Greensboro

Camera Registry Program to aid in crime prevention and investigation.


When a crime occurs, the registered cameras in the vicinity can assist the police in

collecting video evidence. This program enables the Greensboro Police Department to

contact registrants, requesting them to check their surveillance systems for specific dates

and times related to criminal activity. Registration is voluntary, with no associated costs,

and it does not grant direct access to your camera. You can delete your registration at any

time, and your personal information will be confidential unless a court order mandates

disclosure.


The Greensboro Police Department will only contact registrants if a criminal incident

occurs near their security camera. If necessary, law enforcement officers may request a

copy of relevant video footage to aid in the investigation.



Terms and Conditions of Use


By participating in the Connect Greensboro Camera System Registry Program, registrants

hereby represent, understand, consent, and agree to the following:


• The registrant is the owner or authorized person in charge of the camera system identified

herein and authorized to grant access to the Greensboro Police Department for video

retrieval.


• Any footage containing or related to criminal activity may be collected by the Greensboro

Police Department for use as evidence during any stage of a criminal proceeding.


• The registrant understands that the Greensboro Police Department will contact the

registrant directly using the information provided on this site, to request the appropriate

video surveillance footage.


• Under no circumstances shall registrants construe that they are acting as an agent and/or

employee of the City of Greensboro and/or the Greensboro Police Department through the

Program.


• The registrant understands that the City of Greensboro and/or the Greensboro Police

Department are not responsible for any crimes that occur on the registrant’s property.


• The registrant understands that they can delete their registration at any time, and their

personal information will be confidential unless a court order mandates disclosure.


By submitting information, the camera registrant acknowledges and agrees to abide by the

stated policy and terms of use, contributing to the collective effort to deter crime and

enhance public safety in the City of Greensboro.
