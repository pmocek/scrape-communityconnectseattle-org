I. INTRODUCTION


THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (“Memorandum”), is made and entered into on this date between THE TOWN OF QUEEN CREEK POLICE DEPARTMENT (hereinafter referred to as “QCPD”) and RESIDENT OR ORGANIZATION NAME (hereinafter referred to as "Partner").


II. PREAMBLES


WHEREAS, Department is a law enforcement organization responsible for the preservation of safety and security in the geographic area in which the Department has jurisdiction.


WHEREAS, Partner is a resident or organization operating within the jurisdiction of Department.


WHEREAS, this Agreement has as its objective the collaboration and participation of both organizations for the safety and security of those who are at Partner’s location(s), and for this reason this Agreement facilitates the establishment of channels of communication and sharing of video sources that permit the creation and interchange of information, as well as collaboration with the expressed goal of providing enhanced responsiveness and situational awareness for Department on behalf of Partner.


WHEREAS, the missions of the Partners are complementary;


THEREFORE, the Partners wish to work together and in compliance with the following clauses:


III. GOAL


The goal of this agreement is to set forth guidelines for sharing of Partner’s video sources with QCPD for investigative and public safety matters.


IV. PURPOSE


The Community Connect program is designed for residents and organizations within the Town of Queen Creek to share with police investigators the location of the Partner’s security cameras. In an event of a public safety incident or criminal act, investigators can quickly communicate with the Partner who have shared camera asset locations through the Community Connect program.

Investigators can request from the Partner potential video evidence that may be helpful in enhancing an investigation, identifying potential criminal suspects or create investigative leads. Participating residents or organizations are not compelled to share video data with the police department. If video data is voluntarily turned over to QCPD investigators, the collected data will be housed in the department’s secure evidence database. All evidence obtained by QCPD will be subject to Arizona Public Records Laws.


Video sharing by Partner with Department is intended to be done in the spirit of partnership for improved situational awareness and efficiency of police investigations.


V. RESPONSIBILITIES OF QCPD


QCPD will only have access to video data shared by the Partner transferred into the Department’s evidence collection software. Further, QCPD will ensure video access is strictly limited to personnel responsible for monitoring the system, and authorized login metadata will be tracked and logged. Finally, QCPD will ensure any employee responsible for video access is trained on system use and security of access. QCPD will direct any inquiries related to Partner or Partner’s video sources to the appropriate designee as indicated in Section VIII.


The parties acknowledge that QCPD may be subject to certain laws or ordinances which require disclosure of recordings obtained by QCPD to the public and the partner agrees that certain materials may be disclosed as required by law, pursuant to lawful requests.


VI. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTNER

Partner will provide QCPD at least one point of contact, as indicated below, with a basic understanding of Partner’s inventory and locations of surveillance cameras as may be required for video sharing.
