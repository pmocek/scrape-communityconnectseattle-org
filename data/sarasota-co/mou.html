MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 

 

THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (“MOU”), executed on this date between theSARASOTA COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE; hereafter referred to as “SHERIFF”, andPARTNER, an individual, resident, business or other organization with its/their principal address indicated within the Registry portal, hereafter referred to as “PARTNER”; collectively referred to as “THE PARTIES”, for the purpose of facilitating the registrations of Partner's camera systems. 

 

WITNESSETH:

 

WHEREAS, Sheriff is a law enforcement organization responsible for public safety and security in the geographic area in which the Sheriff has jurisdiction.

 

WHEREAS, Partner is an individual, resident, business or organization that owns/operates video surveillance systems located within the jurisdiction of the Sheriff.​

 

WHEREAS, both Parties desire to collaborate for the purpose of increased safety and security of their community and for this reason this MOU facilitates the registration of Partner's cameras between Partner and Sheriff. 

 

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, do agree as follows: 

  

I.  GOAL

 

The goal of this MOU is to set forth guidelines for the registration of Partner's camera systems.

 

 II. PURPOSE

 

This MOU is solely to facilitate the registration of Partner's camera systems, in order to assist the Sheriff with criminal investigations and emergency response. 

 

III.  RESPONSIBILITIES OF SHERIFF

 

Sheriff will not disclose Partner’s registration information with anyone outside of the Sarasota County Sheriff's Office, without the prior written consent of Partner, or as otherwise required by law. Registry information will only be disclosed to authorized law enforcement personnel unless otherwise required by Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, or s. 24, Art. I of the Florida Constitution. 

 

This MOU is subject to Florida’s Public Records Act, Chapter 119 of the Florida Statutes (“Public Records Act”). If any portions of the MOU regarding confidential information are inconsistent with the terms and conditions of the Public Records Act, the Public Records Act shall control. 

 

IF PARTNER HAS QUESTIONS REGARDING THE APPLICATION OF THE PUBLIC RECORDS ACT TO PARTNER’S DUTY TO PROVIDE PUBLIC RECORDS RELATING TO THE AGREEMENT, PARTNER SHALL CONTACT SCSO’S CUSTODIAN OF RECORDS: By Email: Records@sarasotasheriff.org; By Phone: (941)861-4025.

 

IV. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTNER

 

Partner will provide the Sheriff at least one point of contact, as indicated below, with a basic understanding of Partner’s inventory and locations of surveillance cameras.   

 

V. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

 

IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, PUNITIVE OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES OF ANY KIND, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO LOST REVENUES, PROFITS, OR GOODWILL, FOR ANY MATTER ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THE PERFORMANCE OR NONPERFORMANCE OF THIS AGREEMENT. EXCEPT AS CAUSED BY ITS ACTS OR OMISSIONS, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT ALLOWED BY LAW, SHERIFF DISCLAIMS ALL LIABILITY FOR ANY THIRD-PARTY ACCESS TO THE CAMERA, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS, DATA BREACHES, OR PRIVACY VIOLATIONS. IN NO EVENT SHALL SHERIFF BE LIABLE FOR ANY DAMAGES, DIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL, ARISING OUT OF SUCH ACCESS.

 

Nothing in the MOU shall be deemed to be a waiver of any defense of sovereign immunity or any rights or immunities afforded SCSO by section 768.28, Florida Statutes, or any other applicable law, including limits on attorney’s fees, court costs, and other forms of relief. 

 

VI. TERM AND TERMINATION 

 

This MOU shall take effect upon signing by both Parties and shall remain in effect for a period of twelve (12) months.  The MOU term shall auto-renew for an additional twelve (12) month term thereafter, unless terminated for convenience by either Party at any time upon written notice to the other Party. Neither party may assign or transfer all or any portion of this MOU without the prior written consent of the other party. 

 

VII. MISCELLANEOUS 

 

The relationship between the Parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing contained herein shall be construed as creating any agency, partnership, joint venture, or other form of joint enterprise, employment, or fiduciary relationship between the Parties; and neither Party shall have the authority to contract or bind any other Party in any manner whatsoever. This MOU creates no right of exclusivity. 

This MOU is for the benefit of the Parties hereto and is not entered into for the benefit of any other person or entity. Nothing in this MOU shall be deemed or construed to create or confer any benefit, right, or cause of action for any third party or entity.
