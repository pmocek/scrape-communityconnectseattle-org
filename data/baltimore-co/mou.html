VOLUNTARY SECURITY CAMERA REGISTRATION PROGRAM


Owners of commercial or residential private property in Baltimore County, Maryland may voluntarily register private security cameras so the Baltimore County Police Department can more easily contact owners to request video for law enforcement or emergency purposes. To register, an owner must agree to the Registry Terms and Conditions that have been approved by the Chief of Police, Baltimore County Police Department. 


The Baltimore County Police Department has contracted with Axon Enterprise, Inc. (“Axon”) to manage the Voluntary Security Camera Registration Program. Axon will accept registrations on their Baltimore County program webpage. 


The Baltimore County Code authorizes a Voluntary Security Camera Registration Program: 


·      The purpose of the program is to promote public safety, deter crime, and assist law enforcement investigations by establishing a registry, maintained by the police department, of property owners who register their security cameras.  Baltimore County Code, Section 13-11-301.


·      An owner of commercial or residential private property may voluntarily register one or more of their private security cameras on a form provided by the County.  Registrants shall provide contact information and camera locations.  Registrants acknowledge that the police department may use the contact information on the registry to request camera footage and data in the course of a law enforcement investigation. Section 13-11-302.


·      Registration records retained by the police department shall be kept confidential to the extent permitted by law. Section 13-11-303. To ensure the registry remains accurate, registration shall expire after two years, after which the property owner may renew the registration. Section 13-11-304.


REGISTRY TERMS AND CONDITIONS:


Definitions: 


“PARTNER” is the owner of commercial or residential private property in Baltimore County, Maryland who wishes to voluntarily register security cameras with the Baltimore County Police Department.


“AGENCY” is the Baltimore County Police Department, the agency with primary law enforcement jurisdiction in Baltimore County, Maryland.


1.     Partner will provide its principal mailing address (in case the principal mailing address is different from the physical location).

2.     Partner will provide the address of the Partner’s private property in Baltimore County where the Partner has one or more security camera that records video.

3.     Partner will voluntarily provide at least one point of contact so the Agency may contact the Partner in the future to request recorded video and related data for law enforcement or emergency purposes.

4.     To ensure the registry remains accurate, Partner’s registration will expire after two years; the Agency or its designee may contact Partner to request that Partner renew the registration.

5.     The Agency will keep Partner camera locations and contact information confidential to the extent permitted by law.

6.     The Agency will not have direct access to Partner’s cameras or stored video unless Partner is an owner of commercial property who voluntarily joins the separate Integration Program by obtaining, installing and operating an Axon fususCore device.

7.     The Agency makes no commitment to request or view video from Partner’s cameras, or to use submitted information except for the stated purpose of contacting Partner to make a request.

8.     Partner may terminate participation in the Registry at any time.

9.     The Agency may terminate the Voluntary Security Camera Registration Program at any time.

10.  The Agency may modify these Terms and Conditions at any time, after giving reasonable advance notification to Partners.

11.  The Agency’s Voluntary Security Camera Registration Program may not be construed to give any rights or benefits to any person, entity, or agency.
