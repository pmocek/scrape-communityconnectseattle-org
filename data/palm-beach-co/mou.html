III.  GOAL

 

The goal of this agreement is to set forth guidelines for sharing of Partner’s video sources with the Agency.

 

 IV. PURPOSE

 

In the case of Camera Registry, the location of the Partner's video sources is granted to the Agency. Camera registry is completed by Partner for video sources that are owned by or so under management by Partner. Camera registry by Partner is intended to be done in the spirit of partnership for improved situational awareness and efficiency of emergency response by the Agency. Camera registry does not allow the Agency to access Partner’s live video stream. Camera registry provides the Partner contact information to the Agency to assist with direct video evidence collection.

 

 

V.  RESPONSIBILITIES OF DEPARTMENT

 

The Agency will provide registry access is strictly limited to personnel involved with the investigation, and authorized login metadata will be tracked and logged. Finally, Agency will provide any employee responsible for registry access is trained on system use and security of access. The Agency will direct any inquiries related to Partner or Partner’s video sources to the appropriate designee as indicated in Section VIII.

 

VI. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTNER

 

Partner will provide Agency at least one point of contact, as indicated below, with a basic understanding of Partner’s inventory and locations of surveillance cameras as may be required for video sharing.
